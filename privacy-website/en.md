## 2. Data, purposes and legal bases

**Browsing and security.** We process the IP address, date and time, page requested, response code and browser information for the operation, security and diagnostics of the Site. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in ensuring its proper and secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Optional statistics.** We use **Google Analytics 4** only with your consent, to measure visits and interactions, through cookie identifiers, device and browser data, pages visited, traffic source and approximate location. Google does not log or store the IP addresses of European traffic, which are used to derive location. We do not use Google Signals, advertising features or remarketing. The legal basis is consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): you can refuse it with no consequences for your use of the Site, or withdraw it from **“Cookie preferences”** in the footer.

**Cookie choices.** We record a random identifier, date and time, version and language of the policy, categories and the choice made, with no IP address or user-agent in the choice log. We do this to demonstrate consent, in compliance with the obligations under Arts. 5(2) and 7(1) GDPR (Art. 6(1)(c)); for other choices, the basis is legitimate interest in respecting and documenting your preferences (Art. 6(1)(f)).

**Demo and trial requests.** We collect first and last name, company and company type, country, business email, phone number, role, catalogue size, products of interest and any message, to handle the request and get back to you. We also record the date, time, version and language of your declaration that you have read the privacy policy and the Terms, in order to document it.

We send you a confirmation link valid for **24 hours**. Only after confirmation is the request entered in the CRM and handled. Unconfirmed requests are kept exclusively to detect and document abuse, attempted fraud or acts of unfair competition and to protect our rights, including in relation to subsequent requests from the authorities, without any commercial follow-up. If you receive a confirmation message without having submitted a request, you can ignore it and report it to us at **privacy@spaarkly.com**.

The legal basis is the performance of pre-contractual measures taken at your request if you act on your own behalf (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), or legitimate interest in handling the request of the company you represent (Art. 6(1)(f)). To prevent and document abuse, fraud and unfair competition and to document the declarations made, the basis is our legitimate interest in the protection of rights (Art. 6(1)(f)). Mandatory disclosures to the authorities are based on the applicable legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Mandatory fields are marked in the form: without them we cannot handle the request; the message is optional. Activation of the trial in **ARShades Studio** is governed by the platform's Terms and privacy policy.

| Data | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Browsing and security logs | Normally 30 days from recording; longer only to investigate incidents or protect rights, for as long as necessary |
| Google Analytics 4 user and event data | Maximum 14 months; anonymised statistics may be kept longer |
| Log of cookie choices | Up to 26 months after each record is made |
| Cookie choice on your device (`ars_consent`) | 12 months |

| Confirmed requests and related communications | 24 months from the last interaction with you concerning the request; if a contractual relationship arises, the retention periods in the service's privacy policy apply |
| Email delivery logs at ZeptoMail | Normally 60 days from sending |

For transfers to Google LLC in the United States, the **EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework** applies, within the limits of the certification; in other cases, the European Commission's **Standard Contractual Clauses** apply, including those provided for access from outside the EEA by the Zoho group. You can request a copy of the safeguards at **privacy@spaarkly.com**.

## 5. Your rights

In the cases provided for by the GDPR, you can request access to your data and its rectification, erasure, restriction and portability by writing to **privacy@spaarkly.com**. You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing; for analytics, use **“Cookie preferences”**.

**Right to object.** You can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interest, by writing to the same address. We will stop the processing unless there are compelling legitimate grounds which override, or a need for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

You can also lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority; in Italy, this is the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it) (the Italian Data Protection Authority).
