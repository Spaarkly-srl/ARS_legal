# ARSHADES WEBSITE — COOKIE POLICY

*ARShades website* </br> *Version 1.1.0 — Last updated: 21 October 2026 — Effective date: from its publication on the new ARShades website*


This Cookie Policy supplements the [Website Privacy Policy](../privacy-website/en.md) and describes the cookies and similar technologies (including the browser's *localStorage* and *IndexedDB*) used by the **ARShades website** of **Spaarkly s.r.l.** It concerns solely the website presenting the ARShades solutions; the cookies and technologies used within the ARShades product experiences (for example the Virtual Try-On launched by the User) are described in the [Privacy Policy of the ARShades solutions](../privacy-policy/en.md).

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies — such as the browser's *localStorage* and *IndexedDB* — save information in an analogous way. European law (the ePrivacy Directive, art. 122 of the Privacy Code and the GDPR) requires prior consent for every cookie or similar technology that is **not** strictly necessary for the operation of the site or for a service you have requested.

## 2. Categories used

- **Strictly necessary / technical** — indispensable for the operation of the Site: your cookie choice and your theme and language preferences. They do not require consent.
- **Analytics** — measurement cookies of **Google Analytics 4**, used exclusively for aggregate statistics on the Site's usage. **They are installed only if you enable the “Analytics” category in the banner.** Analytics is configured in a privacy-friendly mode: consent denied by default (Google Consent Mode v2), IP address anonymisation active, no Google Signals, no advertising or remarketing functionality, no cross-site profiling.
- **Functional** — data saved in your browser for a feature you use; currently only the photo from the VTO Explorer demo (§4). They are used for the service you have requested, which is why they do not require consent and do not appear in the banner.
- **Marketing** — a category provided for but **currently not used**: the Site installs no advertising or profiling cookies. Should it be introduced, the banner and the table will be updated and consent requested again.

## 3. Legal basis and consent

Technical cookies are based on **legitimate interest** (art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); all other categories are based exclusively on your **consent** (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), collected through the banner on your first visit, with equal prominence between “Accept all” and “Reject all”.

Your choice — including refusal — is stored for **12 months** in the first-party cookie `ars_consent`. A minimal proof of consent (random identifier, timestamp, version and language of the policy shown in the banner, categories and action — **without IP address or user-agent**) is recorded on our servers for the time necessary to demonstrate consent (up to **26 months**) and then automatically removed. In case of refusal, you will not be asked for consent again for at least **6 months**, unless there are substantial changes to the policy.

## 5. Third-party cookies and technologies

The only third party that installs cookies on this Site is **Google LLC** (Google Analytics 4), and only after your consent. Google LLC adheres to the **EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework**; for international data transfers, see the [Website Privacy Policy](../privacy-website/en.md).

The **embedded demos** on the Site's pages (virtual try-on, demo catalogues, VTO Explorer, 3D Viewer, Gateway demo) are ARShades experiences provided by Spaarkly from its own domains (*webvto.it*, *studio.arshades.it*, *api.arshades.com*, *arshades-gateway-demo.web.app*). They do not install cookies on this Site; on their own domain they may save technical data necessary for the experience (for example the language or the open catalogue), without tracking or profiling. The processing within the experiences is described in the [Privacy Policy of the ARShades solutions](../privacy-policy/en.md). The Gateway demo shows a **Google Maps** map: for this service Google LLC acts as an independent data controller ([Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).

## 6. Managing your preferences

- Use the **“Cookie preferences”** link in the footer of every page to review or change your choices at any time; revoking Analytics consent also entails the deletion of the `_ga*` cookies.
- You can delete the VTO Explorer demo photo with the **“Delete photo”** button in the demo.
- You may also delete or block cookies and the Site's data from your browser settings (this may affect technical functions such as the persistence of theme and language).

## 7. Updates to this Cookie Policy

Substantial changes to this Policy (new cookies, new purposes, new third parties) entail a new consent request through the banner. The version of the policy you accepted is recorded together with your choice.
