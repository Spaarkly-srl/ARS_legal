# AR PD METER — PRIVACY IN BRIEF

*ARShades solutions* </br> *Version 1.0 — 13 October 2026 — summary of the ARShades Privacy Policy (26 August 2026) for AR PD Meter users — courtesy translation; the Italian text prevails*

This summary helps you understand the Privacy Policy. It does not replace it: the [full text](../en.md) applies.

- **Who processes your data.** Spaarkly s.r.l., the data controller, which provides AR PD Meter within the website or app you are using.
- **What we do not keep.** Camera images and data about your face: nothing is stored, there is no facial recognition and no facial template is created.
- **What we collect.** Technical data about your session and device (device type, browser, language, and when and how you use the service), to run and improve the service. We keep it for up to 14 months, then anonymise it.
- **Where it is stored.** On servers in the European Union. If any data leaves the European Economic Area (EEA), it does so with the safeguards required by law.
- **Cookies.** Only those strictly necessary for the service to work; no marketing cookies.
- **Your rights.** You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by emailing privacy@spaarkly.com.
