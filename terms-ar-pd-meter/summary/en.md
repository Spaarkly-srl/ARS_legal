# AR PD METER — THE TERMS IN BRIEF

*ARShades solutions* </br> *Version 1.0 — 13 October 2026 — summary of the AR PD Meter Terms and Conditions of Use v1.4 — courtesy translation; the Italian text prevails*

This summary helps you understand the AR PD Meter Terms. It does not replace them: the [full text](../en.md) applies.

- **What it is.** AR PD Meter estimates your pupillary distance (PD) using your device's camera, to help you choose glasses. It is not a medical device.
- **How it works.** Camera images stay on your device: they are not sent or stored, and they are not used to identify you. The technical data derived from them is used only to calculate the measurement and is deleted immediately afterwards.
- **Consent.** By tapping "Get started", you accept the Terms and allow AR PD Meter to use your camera. You can stop at any time by closing the measurement or turning off camera access.
- **Accuracy.** The measurement is an estimate: lighting, distance and movement affect it. For prescription lenses, have your PD checked by an optician or optometrist.
- **Age.** The service is for people aged 18 or older. If you are under 18, use it with a parent or guardian.
- **Who provides it.** Spaarkly s.r.l. (Italy). For questions, email legal@spaarkly.com.
